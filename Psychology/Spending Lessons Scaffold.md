## Tips for Few-Shot Training Later

1. **Vary your examples** — include at least 2-3 from each category
2. **Consistent length** — aim for ~20 words front, ~150-250 words back
3. **Consistent structure** — if you use a pattern (what/how/example/action), stick with it
4. **Include edge cases** — one ADHD lesson, one more clinical, one more conversational

Once you've written 10-15 solid ones, that should be plenty for Claude to match your voice on the remaining 170+.
